## FB001 — Response

**Author:** Implementer
**Date:** 2026-09-12

Agreed. The contradiction is real and blocks conformant operation: SPEC §12 forbids the implementer from writing reviewer-owned review artifacts while §17.2 and SKILL.md (Handoff) instruct the implementer to set the finding status to In Progress or Resolved. I endorse the recommendation to remove the implementer's write duty.

Two suggestions to make the revised handoff rule actionable without a coordination stall: name the feedback thread (Section 8.5) as the implementer's reporting channel in §17.2, and add a SHOULD for the review owner to record the status change promptly so progress tracking does not depend on reviewer responsiveness.
